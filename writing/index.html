Writing

Notes on learning, in progress

I write to think — mostly about how people actually learn, and occasionally about building a career across languages and borders. This is a work in progress; the themes below are the ones I keep returning to.

Learning people finish

Why completion is a design problem, not a motivation problem — and where courses, cohorts, and programmes quietly lose people. What I've learned facilitating working adults who have every reason to drop off.

Learning across cultures

Designing for learners who move between languages and expectations. Notes from advising students bound overseas and running an international university network — where a good experience has to translate, not just be translated.

The craft of facilitation

What actually happens in the room — how to open, when to step back, and how to let a group teach itself. The unglamorous parts of learning design that decide whether it lands.

Pieces are on the way. In the meantime, I share shorter notes on LinkedIn — and I'm always happy to talk shop by email.
